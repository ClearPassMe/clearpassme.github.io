In memory of Yohai Azulai Murdered at the Nova festival, Re'im · October 7, 2023

"Hi, I'm Yohai Azulai,
and I'm going to tell you about myself"

That's how a page in Yohai's diary begins. He wrote about two voices inside him: one that doubts, and one that tells him he has it all - courage, spirit, connection and love. He wrote about striving for truth, about the bond between different people, and about the desire to live a life full of meaning. He went out to dance at the Nova festival, and never came back.

When we read his words, we felt we would have wanted to be his friends. When we met his family, we felt at home. A warm, close, vibrant family, doing everything it can to keep going, despite the pain.

Yohai loved life, experiences and people, and that is the spirit that guides our work.

May his memory be a blessing

About Next October

Our connection to Yohai came through Next October, a commemoration initiative founded by Izhar Shay after his son Yaron was killed on October 7. Its goal: to establish an Israeli startup in memory of each of the fallen, connecting every company with a family and with the story of someone who is no longer here. Alongside commemoration, the initiative supports its companies with mentorship, investor connections and a supportive community. If you are a startup or an organization that would like to take part, we would be glad to have you join.
